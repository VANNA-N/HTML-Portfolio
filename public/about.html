About Me

As a professional aquatic ecologist and fisheries scientist, I combine a robust academic foundation—holding a PhD in Aquatic Ecology, an MSc in Aquatic Resources Management, and a BSc in Fisheries Science—with extensive technical expertise in data analysis and modeling. My research focuses on large-scale spatial and temporal datasets, with a proven track record of publishing in peer-reviewed journals. I am highly proficient in R programming, leveraging advanced statistical techniques such as Generalized Additive Models (GAM) and Random Forest regression, alongside SQL and Microsoft Access for managing and querying extensive environmental databases. This analytical rigor is central to my work in assessing dam-induced environmental changes, where I integrate complex assessments of water quality, fish community structure, and reproductive shifts—including vital fish larvae results—to provide actionable insights for sustainable resource management.
